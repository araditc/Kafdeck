# Code of Conduct

Kafdeck is committed to a professional, respectful, and technically constructive open-source community.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:
- discuss ideas and reviews respectfully,
- critique technical work rather than people,
- provide actionable feedback,
- assume good faith while still enforcing project standards,
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and personal attacks,
- respect security disclosure rules and privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or demeaning language, deliberate intimidation, doxxing/private-information disclosure, repeated bad-faith disruption, and retaliation against good-faith reporting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may moderate, edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions or participation that violates this policy, proportionate to severity and recurrence. Serious incidents may lead to temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Reports involving a maintainer should be escalated to another maintainer/project owner where possible. Security vulnerabilities should follow `SECURITY.md`, not this process.

This concise project policy may later be replaced by an adopted standard community code through normal governance.
