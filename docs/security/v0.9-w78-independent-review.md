## Owner decision

On 2026-11-11, the project owner explicitly accepted KAF-02 conditionally in Codex chat.

> پذیرش مشروط به egress آزموده‌شده

Each supported deployment must enforce and test egress for its actual hosts, CNI and topology. The operator verifies broker-advertised endpoints as well as bootstrap connectivity. Other CNIs, native-host firewall rules, Service DNAT, additive NetworkPolicies and same-node exceptions require their own deployment admission. This decision preserves the complete 0.9 scope and the enforcement requirement. Application-level socket filtering is not claimed.

The independent technical packet requires adoption by the responsible security maintainer through the repository review process. Final artifact, secret-scan, signature and offline-install evidence remain separate acceptance inputs under [W78](https://github.com/araditc/Kafdeck/issues/432).
