All lists/exports require scoped authorization, bounded pagination and safe text rendering. No dashboard, locale, import, catalog link or approval may imply execution authority. Empty/loading/denied/stale/conflict/unsupported/unavailable/unknown states must be distinguishable in actual browser journeys. Telemetry is aggregate and excludes request material containing protected data or credentials.
