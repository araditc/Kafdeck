## Security/read-only invariants

- no Kafka mutation route/control,
- no record produce/consume/payload browser,
- deployment token is not operator identity,
- OIDC mode cannot fall back to Token-mode authentication,
- authorization is backend-authoritative and default-deny,
- secret/OIDC token/session-cookie material is excluded from API/log/audit,
- Kafka ACL denial remains distinct from Kafdeck RBAC denial.
