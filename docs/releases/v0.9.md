# Kafdeck v0.9 candidate

Kafdeck 0.9.0 is a release candidate for the governance and enterprise hardening work tracked in [W72–W82](https://github.com/araditc/Kafdeck/issues/425). The [installation and operator guide](../operator/v0.9-installation-guide.md) covers local evaluation, Helm, sign-in, Kafka connections and upgrade preparation. [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/araditc/Kafdeck/releases) records the published version.

- Governance and platform flows cover catalog, policy, requests and attribution.
- Operator UI work includes route admission and English and Persian accessibility journeys.
- Distribution evidence includes tested Linux amd64 and arm64 image archives and the exact chart used for the Kubernetes lifecycle test. [Candidate artifacts](https://github.com/araditc/Kafdeck/actions/runs/42899843799)
- Full-corpus load measurements cover SQLite and PostgreSQL with 1, 10 and 50 concurrent clients and two-hour soaks. [Proposed performance budgets](../performance/v0.9-budget-protocol.md#measured-candidate)

Review the [distribution and recovery plan](../operations/v0.9-distribution-recovery.md) before upgrading from v0.8. Final platform admission, signed offline media, independent review and budget enforcement are tracked in the release acceptance plan.

The release decision is tracked in [W82](https://github.com/araditc/Kafdeck/issues/436).
