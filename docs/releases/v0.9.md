## Included capabilities

- Ownership catalog, governance policy, service requests/approvals and measured attribution with scoped UI/API/CLI enforcement.
- Managed first-owner setup and user/cluster assignment; separate dashboard pages, stable background refresh, topic details and bounded manual publish/read, consumer members/lag/offset reset.
- Per-connection Schema Registry, Kafka Connect and ksqlDB address/authentication settings; schema versions/compatibility/registration, governed connector lifecycle, and bounded SELECT with earliest/latest reads. Direct Stream/Table creation retains owner/policy/cluster-identity restrictions.
- English/Persian, keyboard/contrast/assistive-technology evidence and explicit Unknown/Blocked/Unsupported semantics.
