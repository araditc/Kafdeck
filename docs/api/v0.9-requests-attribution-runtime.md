## Enablement and authority

`Kafdeck:Governance:RequestsEnabled` and `AttributionEnabled` default to false. Enabling either requires OIDC, governed mutation persistence and an explicit outside `DeploymentGeneration`. Authority stores remain registered when management endpoints are disabled; disabling the request UI never disables an active policy's request requirement. Schema/generation initialization completes before HTTP workers start. The existing offline restore-generation admission and worker fencing still apply.

Existing persisted action numbers 1–67 and mutation kinds 1–34 remain unchanged. New actions are RequestRead 68, RequestSubmit 69, RequestDecide 70, AttributionRead 71 and RateCardManage 72. New kinds are ServiceRequestChange 35, RateCardVersion 36, RateCardActivation 37 and ServiceRequestDecision 38. Request writes are Moderate; rate-card writes and activation are High. Underlying effects retain their original risk, confirmation, current authorization and independent critical approval.

All routes below use `/api/v1/governance`. Browser writes retain cookie/CSRF protection. Non-browser clients require their current action-specific registration as well as current backend RBAC. Service identities can submit their own requests but cannot make human business decisions. Catalog ownership and allocation weights never grant access.

## Immutable requests and separate execution state

`GET /requests?clusterId=...`, `GET /requests/{id}?clusterId=...` and `GET /requests/{id}/history?clusterId=...` expose scoped, bounded request material and immutable history. A request ID is a server-issued lowercase UUID in N format. Lists use signed scope/actor-bound continuation and authorization before pagination; hidden rows do not create visible counts or continuation.

`POST /requests/changes/preview` accepts `{clusterId,requestId?,expectedVersion,command,operationId?,reason,expiresAtUtc?}` for Draft, Revise, Submit or Cancel under RequestSubmit. `POST /requests/decisions/preview` uses the same closed DTO for Approve or Reject under RequestDecide. Both require an idempotency key. The standard mutation confirmation/execution protocol commits each proposed metadata change. `GET /requests/plans/{operationId}` returns the immutable request proposal together with its governed operation.

The business revision binds the original requester/client, existing underlying operation ID, exact preview hash, canonical material hash, policy-precondition digest, outside generation and expiry. It stores safe target summaries rather than protected execution payloads. A changed operation/material/policy requires an explicit new revision and new decision. Version is the CAS token for every business change; revision identifies immutable material. New revisions preserve earlier material and decisions in history.

Business state is Draft, Submitted, Approved, Rejected, Cancelled, Expired or NeedsReplan. Execution state is projected separately from the existing kernel. One current scoped human RequestDecide decision, distinct from the requester across browser and CLI identities, can approve a submitted revision. An eligible rejection closes that revision. Business approval neither confirms the operation nor supplies its independent critical approval.

The policy gate reads verified request evidence on the effect's existing database transaction. It rechecks immutable operation binding, generation, expiry and current requester/reviewer authority. All clients, direct routes and resumed workers use this gate. Caller JSON, explain fixtures and prompts cannot supply an approval fact. Cancel, expiry or rejection cannot clear an executing/unknown effect or authorize replay; positive observation/reconciliation remains governed by the existing operation protocol.

## Acquisition, provenance and historical ownership

`Kafdeck:Governance:Attribution` configures `Sources`, `PollSeconds` (60–3,600) and `RetentionDays` (default 90, maximum 366). A source registration contains SourceId, ClusterId, Provider, a fixed HTTPS Endpoint and an optional env:/file: AccessTokenReference. The registered provider is `KafdeckMeasuredCountersV1`. There is no caller URL, arbitrary PromQL/query proxy or public measurement-ingest endpoint.

Each collection tick isolates non-shutdown source failures so one unavailable source cannot starve healthy sources or retention. Shutdown cancellation remains cancellation. Measurements, attribution windows and dashboards require both AttributionRead and underlying TopicRead, plus any linked catalog-owner visibility, before pagination or aggregation.

Acquisition reuses pinned DNS/egress, HTTPS certificate/hostname checks and secret-reference handling. Responses are closed schema-version-1 JSON, at most 1 MiB, depth 12 and 500 windows. Each counter window identifies a resource, metric/unit, UTC start/end, start/end counter values, coverage, source revision and optional physical resource identity. Admitted pairs are trafficBytes/bytes and storageByteSeconds/byteSeconds. The quantity is the measured counter delta; reset, missing/incomplete coverage and stale evidence remain explicit unknown quantities. These registered source measurements do not establish Kafka broker billable throughput, which must not be inferred from lag, record length or samples.

Ownership is resolved from actual catalog history for the measurement interval and physical topic identity. The window records the ownership revision and provenance hash. A later reassignment cannot rewrite the earlier observation. An assignment boundary or insufficient identity/interval evidence yields ambiguity/missing ownership rather than an invented split.

## Bounded reads, allocation and rate cards

`GET /measurements`, `/attribution` and `/dashboards` require clusterId, fromUtc and toUtc; resourceId is optional. Query intervals range from one minute to 31 days. Lists default to 50/max 200 records and use explicit continuation, within the admitted 500-window response ceiling. Current AttributionRead and underlying resource visibility apply before aggregation and pagination. Coverage and totals explicitly distinguish a complete interval from page-only/available-measurement results; an empty page is not a zero bill.

Each cluster admits at most 10,000 distinct permission-relevant measurement scopes: exact resource ID plus historical team/application ownership. This is a scope-inventory ceiling, not a window-count ceiling. Ingestion checks it transactionally; authorized scope IDs are selected before the SQL window limit. Large hidden histories cannot consume a visible page budget, change its continuation or reveal a hidden row count. Retention reclaims an inventory entry only after every referring measurement expires; request/rate/audit evidence is retained.

A direct, unambiguous single-owner quantity is Observed. Shared allocation uses explicit versioned weights and is Estimated; absent weights never imply an equal split. Allocated quantities plus explicit residual equal the measured quantity in decimal arithmetic. Counter reset, missing rate, stale evidence, owner ambiguity and incomplete coverage keep null/unknown estimates and their reasons.

`GET /rate-cards/versions`, `/rate-cards/state` and `/rate-cards/operations/{id}` expose authorized versions/state/material. `POST /rate-cards/versions/preview` accepts clusterId and a document containing metricKind, unit, currency, decimal-string amount, effectiveFromUtc/effectiveToUtc and explicit weights. `POST /rate-cards/activations/preview` binds clusterId, versionId, expectedRevision and enabled. Both use High governed confirmation and idempotency. Distinct metric/unit cards can coexist; overlapping active intervals for the same unit/currency are rejected. Versions and activation receipts remain immutable after disablement.

An estimate binds the window, ownership and rate-card hashes. Quantities, weights and amounts use invariant decimal strings. Totals sum unrounded evidence and round once to two displayed decimal places with midpoint-to-even. Negative rates, overflow and mixed-currency totals are rejected. There is no foreign exchange, settlement, payment or billing authority.

New version commits and activations recheck configured team/application references against the active catalog on their owning transaction. Archiving an owner between preview and execution cannot introduce a new stale allocation binding. Previously committed historical measurements, rates and positive receipts retain their evidence.

## Clients, discovery and recovery

UI panels and CLI families `requests`, `measurements`, `attribution`, `dashboards` and `rate-cards` use these same backend contracts. Request CLI change-preview selects the decision endpoint for Approve/Reject; an automation profile cannot silently open a human decision flow. Actual route metadata generates OpenAPI 3.1 discovery at `/api/v1/openapi/v0.9-governance.json`, including required time-range queries.

Both new write families store positive operation/material-bound transaction receipts. A lost acknowledgement stays ExecutionUnknown until explicit `POST /requests/operations/{id}/recover` or `/rate-cards/operations/{id}/recover` proves the commit under current authority, outside generation and guarded operation CAS. These POSTs are bodyless. Recovery cannot infer success from absence or dispatch again. Catalog/policy/request audit and unresolved evidence are not evicted by measurement retention.
